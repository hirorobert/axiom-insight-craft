A plan is a one-time payment for a 12-month term and does not renew automatically, so nothing needs to be cancelled to stop a future charge. You may cancel a plan before its term ends by writing to billing@cfoclose.com and ask for a refund of the unused part of the term, calculated pro rata by the whole days remaining from the day we receive your request. Refunds are made through Polar, our merchant of record, to the original payment method. A refund is decided and made by a person at CFOClose, never automatically, and does not change access by itself: we review the plan's access and tell you the outcome. A plan cancelled with a refund ends when the refund is made; work already prepared stays readable to the account holder. A dispute raised with your card issuer is handled through Polar.
