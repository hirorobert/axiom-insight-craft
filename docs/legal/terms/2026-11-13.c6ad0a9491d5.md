Legal notices: contact@cfoclose.com . 11. QuickBooks connections and imports Customer QuickBooks connections are not currently available. The integration is being tested with Intuit sandbox companies. When available, only connect books you are authorized to access. Authorization takes place at Intuit; CFOClose does not collect your Intuit password. Intuit grants an accounting scope that is broader than read-only access. CFOClose restricts its connector to reading the trial balance and fiscal preferences, together with the token operations needed to connect, refresh access and disconnect. It does not write accounting transactions back to QuickBooks. An import is a snapshot, not continuous synchronization. Review its company, reporting period, accounting basis, currency, balances and classifications before relying on it. QuickBooks trial balances use fiscal year-to-date semantics; the connector checks fiscal settings against your confirmed reporting dates. Disconnecting stops new imports and requests revocation of access. Saved imports and reports remain in your workspace; disconnection is not a deletion request. An unconfirmed revocation must not be treated as completed. 12. Optional AI assistance Customer-facing AI features are currently disabled or withheld server-side.
