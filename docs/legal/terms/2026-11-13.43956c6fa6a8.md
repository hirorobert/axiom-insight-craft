What CFOClose is today CFOClose is accounting-workflow software for audit and finance teams. The service available to customers today is trial balance review: upload and validate a trial balance, review and confirm its account classifications, and reach a reviewed trial balance ready for statement preparation. Each review decision is recorded against the signed-in user who made it. A workspace whose filing jurisdiction has a jurisdiction pack may also show that jurisdiction's own record-keeping panel. Financial reporting is a pilot by invitation: it is offered only to companies we have invited, it is not generally available, and its accounting has not been independently validated. Other parts of the software are not available to customers. CFOClose assists a firm's own professionals — it does not replace professional judgment, and it is not itself a licensed audit or accounting firm. 2. Accounts and access You need an account to use the service. You are responsible for the accuracy of information you submit and for restricting access to your account and your firm's workspace to people you have authorized. 3. Plans, payment and term Plans and their prices are listed on the plans page . Each plan is bought by a one-time card payment for a 12-month term. A plan does not renew automatically: nothing is charged again unless you buy a new term. Card payments are processed by Polar, acting as merchant of record: Polar takes the payment on its own secure page, adds any sales tax that applies to your location and shows the total before you pay. Your card details never reach CFOClose. Polar's own terms apply to the payment. A plan starts only when the payment has been verified with Polar, not when you return from the payment page. An upgrade starts when its payment is verified and ends your current plan at that moment; unused time on the current plan is not refunded automatically, and you can contact us to have it credited. Connecting an accounting source does not itself authorize a purchase or change your plan. When a term ends, the plan ends. Nothing is deleted: the account holder can still read the work already prepared, while team members' access and actions that need a current plan, such as adding an entity, stop until a new term is bought. The end date is shown in your billing settings; we do not currently send a reminder before a term ends. 4. Refunds and cancellation A plan is a one-time payment for a 12-month term and does not renew automatically, so nothing needs to be cancelled to stop a future charge. You may cancel a plan before its term ends by writing to billing@cfoclose.com and ask for a refund of the unused part of the term, calculated pro rata by the whole days remaining from the day we receive your request. Refunds are made through Polar, our merchant of record, to the original payment method. A refund is decided and made by a person at CFOClose, never automatically, and does not change access by itself: we review the plan's access and tell you the outcome. A plan cancelled with a refund ends when the refund is made; work already prepared stays readable to the account holder. A dispute raised with your card issuer is handled through Polar. 5. We do not sell your data. See the Privacy Policy for how it is handled. 6. No warranty of statutory accuracy Accounting rules applied by the service are updated on a best-effort basis. The service does not warrant that every computation is free of error or that it reflects the most recent regulatory change at all times. A qualified professional must review outputs before they are relied upon, filed, or submitted to any authority. 7. Changes to these terms We may update these terms as the service changes. QuickBooks connections and imports Customer QuickBooks connections are not currently available. The integration is being tested with Intuit sandbox companies. When available, only connect books you are authorized to access. Authorization takes place at Intuit; CFOClose does not collect your Intuit password. Intuit grants an accounting scope that is broader than read-only access. CFOClose restricts its connector to reading the trial balance and fiscal preferences, together with the token operations needed to connect, refresh access and disconnect. It does not write accounting transactions back to QuickBooks. An import is a snapshot, not continuous synchronization. Review its company, reporting period, accounting basis, currency, balances and classifications before relying on it. QuickBooks trial balances use fiscal year-to-date semantics; the connector checks fiscal settings against your confirmed reporting dates. Disconnecting stops new imports and requests revocation of access. Saved imports and reports remain in your workspace; disconnection is not a deletion request. An unconfirmed revocation must not be treated as completed. 10. Optional AI assistance Customer-facing AI features are currently disabled or withheld server-side. Trial balance ingestion and financial calculations use deterministic code. Optional AI functionality in the code is advisory only; it does not approve accounts, journals or reports. Before enabling external AI processing of accounting data, we require a review of provider data-handling terms, appropriate disclosures and authorization controls.
