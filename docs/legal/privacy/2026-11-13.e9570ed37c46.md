What we collect Account information you provide (name, email, firm and company names), the trial balances and supporting documents you upload, the review decisions, reports and other engagement records your firm produces while using the service (including records created with services that are no longer offered), commercial records needed to operate your plan (see section 4), and basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the service. 2. How it's stored Data is stored in our hosted database and file storage, encrypted at rest, behind authentication and row-level access controls scoped to your firm's own companies and workspace members. Staff do not access your accounting data except as needed to operate, secure, or support the service, or to comply with the law. These are the practices we follow; we do not claim a certified compliance posture (such as GDPR, ISO 27001, or SOC 2) unless and until we hold that certification. 3. How it's used To provide the service you signed up for: checking and reviewing the trial balances you upload and recording your review decisions (and, for a company invited to the financial reporting pilot, preparing its reports), maintaining your workspace state, and keeping an audit trail of professional decisions and administrative actions for your own firm's record. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your accounting data to train models for other customers. 4. Commercial and billing data Separately from your accounting data, we maintain minimal commercial records (plan, licence status and, where applicable, payment history and provider payment references — never card numbers) needed to operate accounts and support. This commercial record is kept administratively separate from, and is never used to alter, your accounting data or the results your firm has produced. 5. Retention and deletion We keep your account and workspace data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete or export your data by writing to support@cfoclose.com ; we first confirm your authority over the account. After a verified deletion request we delete or anonymise your personal data, except records the law requires us to keep (such as payment and billing records) and records of signed work where the law or a professional body requires them. Backups held by our hosting provider expire on its backup schedule. 6. Third parties Authorized workspace members can access the company records their permissions allow. Operational staff access is limited to service, security, support and legal needs. These external parties receive personal or financial data when you use the parts of the service named: Supabase - database, sign-in, file storage and server functions: all the data described in section 1. Lovable - site hosting, and the service's transactional e-mail (sign-up confirmation, password reset, invitations and enquiry acknowledgements), delivered through Mailgun: the recipient's e-mail address and the message. Google - only if you choose “Continue with Google”: you sign in at Google, and your name and e-mail address are passed to CFOClose; and the fonts the site loads from Google, which receives your browser's IP address and browser details when a page loads. Cloudflare Turnstile - a bot check on the public contact form: signals from your browser needed for the check. Namecheap Private Email - hosts the cfoclose.com mailboxes (support, billing and general enquiries): the messages you send us. Polar - for purchases, Polar Software, Inc. acts as merchant of record and processes your payment and billing details as an independent controller under its own privacy terms. We receive your name, e-mail, account reference and the payment outcome, never card numbers. Intuit - authorization and source requests for QuickBooks connections. Customer QuickBooks connections are not currently available (section 8). Optional AI providers are described in section 9. 7. Contact For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or support, email support@cfoclose.com . QuickBooks data Customer QuickBooks access is currently disabled; sandbox testing uses Intuit sample companies. A connection records the source company identifier, environment, connection status and the person who connected it. Refresh tokens are encrypted on the server and are not exposed to workspace browsers. On an authorized import, the connector reads the trial balance and fiscal preferences. Saved records include account names and balances, period, currency, accounting basis, import time and integrity fingerprints. CFOClose does not collect your Intuit password or write accounting transactions back to QuickBooks. Disconnecting stops new imports and requests token revocation. It does not delete previously imported accounting records or reports. You can request deletion through the contact above, subject to applicable legal and audit-record retention obligations. 9. AI processing Customer-facing AI features are currently disabled or withheld by server controls. The code includes optional Anthropic and Lovable AI Gateway integrations, including Google Gemini. These are separate from the development tools used to build CFOClose. Deterministic trial balance ingestion does not call a model. Disabled runtime AI features do not dispatch accounting data to a model provider. We do not use your accounting data to train models for other customers. We have not verified a provider-wide zero-retention or no-training commitment for every optional integration and do not represent that we hold one. Before enabling processing of accounting data by an external model, we require review of the applicable provider terms, retention and training settings, updated disclosures and appropriate authorization controls. 10. Hosting and international processing Personal data is stored and processed outside Tanzania by the service providers listed in section 6, which include providers based in the United States. Content delivery, support and operational processing can involve other locations. We do not promise that all processing is confined to your country. Any future external AI processing requires the review described above. Supabase data residency and transfers describes the provider's regional hosting and international processing practices.
