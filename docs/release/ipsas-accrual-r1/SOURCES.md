Publication dates for IPSAS 45/47/48 are 26 May 2023; IPSAS 43 announcement is 31 January 2022. Routing rejects initial application before these published dates. This is a candidate constraint, not a legal conclusion about every entity's adoption history.

The 2025 text pin does not establish full coverage of subsequent 2026 pronouncements. A current, paragraph-level register and reconciliation against the latest handbook remain mandatory. UN guidance and illustrative financial statements can inform presentation review; neither substitutes for applicable Standards or gives blanket reuse rights.

For a no-early-adoption 30 June year end: FY2025 starts July 2024 (legacy revenue/PPE/leases); FY2026 starts July 2025 (43/45, legacy revenue); FY2027 starts July 2026 (47/48). Comparative transition treatment remains an explicit unresolved task.
