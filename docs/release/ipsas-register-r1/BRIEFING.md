# Briefing for the IPSAS accounting author and independent reviewer

> Draft for the CFOClose team to send, edit or discard. The team decides terms, fees and dates; nothing here appoints or commits anyone.

**To:** Humphrey (accounting author) and Sawe (independent reviewer)
**Subject:** CFOClose, IPSAS accrual: please review how our software treats a first set of requirements

## What CFOClose is, in two sentences

CFOClose is a web product that helps accounting firms in Tanzania turn a trial balance into reviewed financial statements. Before it may tell any user that a report is "prepared in accordance with IPSAS", two qualified accountants, who are not the same person, must have checked, and signed for, the rules the software follows.

## What we are asking

You have been named as the **author** (Humphrey) and the **independent reviewer** (Sawe). Neither of you writes code. The work is reading and judging.

1. We give you a draft register (`DRAFT_REGISTER.md`): about two dozen short rows describing, in plain words, what the software does in three areas: **property, plant and equipment (cost model)**, **revenue and grants by period**, and **presentation of the two main statements**.
2. **Humphrey**, for each row: say when it applies, **write the citation to the standard** (we deliberately left every citation blank; we did not trust ourselves to supply them), say whether the treatment is right, check the worked examples, and mark **Agree**, **Change needed** or **Cannot support**.
3. **Sawe**, for each row: do the same **independently, before reading Humphrey's marks**, then compare and sign.
4. Where you disagree with the software, you tell us; our engineers change it and re-run the tests. Nothing goes live on the strength of a row you have not both signed.

## What we are NOT asking

- You are not endorsing the whole product, only the rows you sign.
- You are not asked to review IFRS for SMEs, tax, or anything outside the three areas above.
- No real client data is involved in this step. The worked examples are synthetic.
- The software already refuses to claim official IPSAS compliance. Signing rows does not switch that on by itself; other conditions remain (see the last section of the register).

## What we need back from each of you

- Full name, professional qualification and registration number.
- **Sawe:** a short written confirmation that you did not write these rows, and that you have no interest in the organisation we later use as the test case.
- Your own estimate of the time the review will take, once you have seen the register.
- Anything in the draft that you think is wrong, missing, or misleadingly worded, even if it is outside your assigned rows.

## What happens after you sign

- Your names appear against the rows you signed. The product may describe a scope as reviewed only to the extent the register says so, and only for the rows signed.
- We then run one timed test with one real organisation and one accountant (the charter's "coaching test") before anything is offered to customers.

## Things to check with the CFOClose team first

- Your engagement terms and fees.
- Your own professional standing: whether putting your name to this work fits your registration and any firm policy. That is for you to confirm with your professional body or firm, not something we can advise on.

Thank you. If anything in the register is unclear, ask: a question about wording is more useful than a silent "agree".
