Step 3 · Reviewed

When all applicable checks have passed and the required decisions are recorded, the trial balance is marked reviewed and ready for statement preparation. In this example that happens only after the two outstanding decisions.

    What "reviewed" means: a reviewed trial balance is balanced and mapped. It is not reconciled to evidence, audited or approved.

    Assurances

    • Traceable source

      A SHA-256 fingerprint is recorded for supported trial-balance imports.

      It identifies the exact file you imported. It is not a signature and not a security certification.NEW

    • Attributed decisions

      Review decisions are associated with authenticated user accounts.

      You can see who decided each account. Your firm still sets its own approval policy.NEW

    • Protected history

      Supported historical outputs remain readable under the defined lifecycle and subscription rules.

      Work you have prepared stays readable if a plan ends; preparing new work needs a current plan.NEW
