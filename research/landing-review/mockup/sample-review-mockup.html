    Assurances

    • Traceable source

      A SHA-256 fingerprint is recorded for supported trial-balance imports.

      It identifies the exact file you imported. It is not a signature and not a security certification.

    • Attributed decisions

      Review decisions are associated with authenticated user accounts.

      You can see who decided each account. Your firm still sets its own approval policy.

    • Protected history

      Supported historical outputs remain readable under the defined lifecycle and subscription rules.

      Work you have prepared stays readable if a plan ends; preparing new work needs a current plan.
